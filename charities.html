Charitable Giving

Funded and directed by our members — because a thriving region is everyone's responsibility.

Our commitment

Leadership, put to work for the community

The mission of The CEO Group is to propose, promote, and support efforts that create opportunity and enhance the quality of life throughout the region. Each year, our members direct meaningful support to the causes that strengthen Cumberland County — classroom scholarships, food security, veterans, healthcare, and youth programs.
