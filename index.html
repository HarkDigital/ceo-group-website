Who we are

The collective CEO voice for our region

Founded in 1996 by Francis J. Reilly, The CEO Group brings together the chief executives of Cumberland County's major private-sector employers around a single idea: that when the region's business leaders speak with one voice, the whole community is stronger for it.

For nearly three decades we have worked side by side on the issues that shape everyday life here - regulation, taxation, workforce development, infrastructure, and a renewed sense of community pride.

“To fashion a concerted effort to fill the vacuum created by the absence of the collective CEO voice in local and regional issues.”

Francis J. Reilly - Founder, The CEO Group

What we do

Four ways we move the region forward

Every initiative flows from a single belief: that engaged leadership, applied together, changes communities.

Regional Advocacy

We bring the collective CEO voice to the issues that matter most - taxation, regulation, infrastructure, and workforce - so the region's interests are heard.

Guest Speakers

Renowned entrepreneurs and corporate leaders share their insight with our members - sharpening the thinking that drives our local economy.

Charitable Giving

Year after year we direct meaningful support to scholarships, food security, veterans, and education across Cumberland County and beyond.

Networking

From golf tournaments to legacy nights, our gatherings build the relationships and trust that turn individual leaders into a unified force.
