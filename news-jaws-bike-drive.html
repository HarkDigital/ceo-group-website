JAWS Youth Playbook Bike Drive

The CEO Group lent its support to the JAWS Youth Playbook, the youth-empowerment foundation led by former Philadelphia Eagles quarterback and longtime CEO Group guest Ron Jaworski.

Among the foundation's signature efforts is its annual bike drive, which puts new bicycles into the hands of deserving children across the region.
