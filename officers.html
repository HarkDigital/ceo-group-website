In memoriam

Honoring our founder

Francis J. Reilly founded The CEO Group in 1996 and served as its guiding voice. His vision — that the region's executives could accomplish together what none could alone — remains the organization's north star. The annual Francis J. Reilly Memorial Golf Tournament and the Reilly Scholarship Fund carry his name forward.
